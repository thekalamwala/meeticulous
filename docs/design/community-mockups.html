05 · First write action

Terms dialog

Shown once, on the first post, reply, like or report. The server rejects writes until accepted (403 COMMUNITY_TERMS_REQUIRED).

Before you post, a few house rules.

  • Be kind. No harassment, hate or sexual content.
  • Keep bookings on Meeticulous. No phone numbers, emails or outside payment links.
  • Don't name and shame. Problems with a session go to Support, not the feed.
  • No spam or ads. Share your work; don't sell in every post.
  • Posts are public. Anyone can read them, and they may show up in search.

Read the full community guidelines, including how to reach our grievance officer.
