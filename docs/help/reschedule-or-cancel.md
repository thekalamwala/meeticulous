# Reschedule or cancel a booking

You can move or cancel your own bookings from your [My Bookings](/my-bookings) page.

## How to reschedule

Open the booking, choose **Reschedule**, and pick a new time from the host's available slots. The host is notified of the change.

Your **first reschedule is always free.** Some hosts set a small reschedule fee (₹10) that applies only from the *second* reschedule onward — if one is due, you'll be asked to pay it before the new time is confirmed. Many hosts charge nothing at all.

## How to cancel

Open the booking and choose **Cancel**. Only the person who made the booking (or the host) can cancel it.

A cancelled booking can't be rescheduled — you'd book a new time instead.

## What about a refund?

Cancelling doesn't automatically refund a payment. Refunds are handled case by case — see [Refunds and failed payments](/help/docs/refunds-and-failed-payments).

## "Reschedule is currently disabled"

Occasionally a host has rescheduling turned off. If you see this and need to change your time, reach us from the [Help](/help) page and we'll sort it out.
