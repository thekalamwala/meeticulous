# Pricing and commission

Full, current prices and limits are always on the [pricing](/pricing) page — this is an overview of how it works.

## The plans

- **Sloth** — free forever, no credit card, with real online payment collection included. A 5% commission applies per booking.
- **Sloth Active** — a flat subscription that drops commission to 0% and adds the Client CRM, more automation and priority support.

## How much does each plan cost?

If you're wondering how much Book A Sloth costs, or what Sloth Active comes to monthly or yearly — the current prices are listed on the [pricing](/pricing) page.

Here's the shape of it:

- **Sloth** costs nothing — it's free forever (a 5% per-booking commission applies).
- **Sloth Active** is ₹399 a month or ₹3,599 a year, GST included — under ₹10 a day, and about 25% cheaper paid yearly.

Prices can change and are set by Book A Sloth, so check the [pricing](/pricing) page for the up-to-date figure.

## Commission

- On **Sloth Active, commission is 0%** — you keep the full service price your client pays.
- On the **free Sloth plan**, a 5% commission applies per booking. That's what funds a genuinely free tier instead of a limited trial — we only earn when you do.

The exact percentage and prices are on the [pricing](/pricing) page.

## Fees on a paid booking

Two costs sit on top of the service price at checkout, and the guest sees the full total before paying:

- A flat **₹10 booking fee (+18% GST)** — charged by Book A Sloth on each paid booking, regardless of plan.
- The **payment gateway's processing fee** (Razorpay's) — charged by the gateway, not by us.

Your earnings are calculated from the service price you set, not the total the guest pays.

## Setting your prices

You choose the price of each service. Paid services have a **minimum price of ₹100**. You can also list free (₹0) services, up to your plan's free-service limit — see [Plans and upgrading](/help/docs/plans-and-upgrading).

## Switching plans

You can upgrade or downgrade as your business changes, with no lock-in beyond the billing period you've already paid for. See [Plans and upgrading](/help/docs/plans-and-upgrading).
