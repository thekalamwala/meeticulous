# Pricing and commission

Full, current prices and limits are always on the [pricing](/pricing) page — this is an overview of how it works.

## The plans

- **Starter** — free forever, no credit card, with real online payment collection included. Commission applies only when you get paid.
- **Pro** — a flat subscription that takes appointment commission to 0% and adds the full Client CRM, guest self-reschedule, more automation, Creator Intelligence and priority support.

## How much does each plan cost?

If you're wondering how much Meeticulous costs, or what Pro comes to monthly or yearly — the current prices are listed on the [pricing](/pricing) page.

Here's the shape of it:

- **Starter** costs nothing — it's free forever. Commission applies per paid sale (below).
- **Pro** is ₹199 a month or ₹1,199 a year, GST included — about ₹3 a day, and 50% cheaper paid yearly (pay for six months, get twelve).
- While founding spots are open, Pro yearly is **₹899**, locked for life. Monthly is ₹199 either way.

Prices can change and are set by Meeticulous, so check the [pricing](/pricing) page for the up-to-date figure.

## Commission

Commission is taken from your side and already includes GST — we send you a commission tax invoice.

| | Starter | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Appointments | 9% | 0%, no monthly limit |
| Digital products, video courses, memberships | 12% | 4% |

On Pro there is no monthly limit: appointments stay at 0% however much you earn.

Pro pays for itself once you take roughly ₹1,110 a month in appointment bookings — that's where Starter's 9% passes Pro's yearly price.

## Fees on a paid booking

- A flat **₹10 booking fee, GST included** — paid by your guest on appointments of **₹300 or more**, on either plan. The ₹10 is ₹8.47 for us plus ₹1.53 GST, and the guest's receipt shows both lines. Appointments under ₹300, free (₹0) services, digital products, video courses and memberships carry **no** booking fee.
- The **payment gateway's processing fee** (Razorpay's) — deducted from your payout, not added to what your guest pays. It's what the gateway actually charges, with no markup.

Your earnings are calculated from the service price you set, not the total the guest pays.

## Setting your prices

You choose the price of each service. Paid services have a **minimum price of ₹100**. You can also list free (₹0) services, up to your plan's free-service limit — see [Plans and upgrading](/help/docs/plans-and-upgrading).

## Switching plans

You can upgrade or downgrade as your business changes, with no lock-in beyond the billing period you've already paid for. See [Plans and upgrading](/help/docs/plans-and-upgrading).
