# Why am I not getting bookings?

If your page is quiet, run through this checklist — most gaps are one of these.

## Is your page ready to book?

- **Profile complete and active** — a half-finished profile doesn't inspire a booking, and some parts of your page only show once it's complete.
- **At least one active service** — a deactivated service won't appear. Check Services and make sure yours are active.
- **Working hours set** — with no availability, there are no slots for guests to pick. Set your hours under Availability.

## Are you sharing your link?

Your booking link (`meeticulous.com/your-name`) is yours to share — put it in your Instagram bio, WhatsApp, email signature, wherever your clients already find you. Meeticulous is a booking tool, not a discovery marketplace, so bookings come from you sharing your link.

## Is your pricing and offer clear?

Guests book faster when the service title, description, and price make it obvious what they're getting. Fill in inclusions and FAQs so there are no question marks.

## Still stuck?

If your page looks complete and you're sharing your link but bookings still aren't landing, reach us from the [Help](/help) page and we'll take a look with you.
