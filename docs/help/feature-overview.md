# What Meeticulous can and can't do

A straight list of what's here today and what isn't yet — so you can decide if it fits, and so you know what to expect.

## What Meeticulous does today

- **One booking page, one link.** Set up your services once and share a single link — `meeticulous.com/your-name` — where clients pick a time and book.
- **Book and pay in one flow.** Clients choose a slot and pay in the same step. Payments run through Razorpay — UPI, cards, and netbanking.
- **Two-way Google Calendar sync.** Confirmed bookings are pushed to your Google Calendar (and updated or removed when they change), and your existing Google busy times are read back in so you're never double-booked.
- **Buffer time between sessions.** You can set grace-period minutes before and/or after a service, so your day isn't wall-to-wall.
- **Automatic confirmations and reminders.** Booking confirmations and reminders go out on WhatsApp and email — you don't send them by hand.
- **Reviews and a verified badge.** Clients can leave reviews, and verified hosts get a badge on their page.
- **Your own branding.** Set your page's theme colour so it looks like you, not a generic template.
- **Digital products, not just appointments.** Sell downloadable products alongside your bookable services.
- **Client CRM and follow-ups (Pro).** Your client list builds itself, with follow-up automation on the Pro plan.
- **GST tax invoices.** Proper GST invoices (CGST/SGST or IGST) are issued for your subscription and bookings. See [GST invoices](/help/docs/gst-invoices).
- **0% commission on Pro.** On Pro you keep the full price your client pays; on the free Starter plan we take 5%, and only when you actually get paid. See [Plans and upgrading](/help/docs/plans-and-upgrading).

## What it doesn't do yet

We'd rather tell you straight than have you find out later.

- **No dedicated mobile app.** There's no iOS or Android app to install — the web app works well in a mobile browser.
- **No Zapier, public API, or webhooks.** The one integration today is Google Calendar. There's no way yet to connect other tools automatically.
- **No international guest payments.** Meeticulous is India-first: payments run through Razorpay and guest sign-in uses an Indian mobile number for the OTP. It works best when your clients are in India.
- **No team or multiple staff.** It's single-host today — one person's services, calendar, and payouts. Multi-staff scheduling and round-robin assignment aren't available yet (on the roadmap).
- **No Outlook or iCloud sync.** Google Calendar is fully two-way, but other calendar providers aren't synced yet.
- **No custom domain.** Your page is your Meeticulous link (`meeticulous.com/your-name`); you can't point your own domain at it yet. See [Custom domain](/help/docs/custom-domain).
- **No recurring or auto-repeat bookings.** Each booking is a one-time appointment — there's no "repeat weekly" option yet.
- **No group sessions.** One guest books one slot; there's no multiple-attendees-per-slot or class-style booking yet.

## "How does this compare to Calendly or Topmate?"

Where Meeticulous lines up: one shareable booking link, two-way Google Calendar sync, payment taken at the moment of booking, buffer time between sessions, and 0% commission on Pro. Where it's behind today: recurring bookings, group sessions, multiple team members, non-Google calendar sync, and custom domains — those aren't shipped yet. See [Plans and upgrading](/help/docs/plans-and-upgrading) for what's included.
