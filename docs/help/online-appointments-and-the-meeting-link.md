# Online appointments and the meeting link

Every service on Meeticulous is either **online** or **in-person**. For an online booking, your meeting happens over a video call — and the link to join is created for you.

## Where is my meeting link? (guests)

For an online booking, open [My Bookings](/my-bookings) and you'll see a **Join** button on the appointment once it's confirmed. Tap it at your appointment time to join the video call.

The same link is on your [booking details](/my-bookings) page, and — because you're added as a guest on the host's calendar invite — it also lands in your Google Calendar for that time. So you can join from your bookings page or straight from your calendar.

The Join button shows for **upcoming** online bookings. Past appointments don't show it.

## How the link gets created (hosts)

When a host has connected Google Calendar, Meeticulous creates a **Google Meet** link automatically the moment an online booking is confirmed. It adds a calendar event for the session, invites the guest, and attaches that Meet link to the booking — no manual step.

If you host online services, connect Google Calendar from your dashboard so links are generated for you. See [Availability and Google Calendar](/help/docs/availability-and-google-calendar).

## I don't see a link

A couple of reasons a link might not be there yet:

- **The service is in-person.** In-person bookings show the location (or a **Get directions** link), not a video link. Check the service to confirm it's set to online.
- **The host hasn't connected Google Calendar.** Without it, Meeticulous can't auto-create the Meet link — the host will share how to join a different way (often in the booking notes, or by messaging you). Hosts: connect Google Calendar to fix this for every future booking.

If it's an online booking, the host's calendar is connected, and you still don't see a link close to your appointment, reach us from the [Help](/help) page and we'll sort it out.

## In-person bookings

If the service is in-person, there's no video link. Your confirmation shows the host's location, and where the host has set one, a **Get directions** link. Everything else — reminders, rescheduling, reviews — works the same as online.
