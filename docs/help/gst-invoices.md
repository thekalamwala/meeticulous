# GST invoices

Yes — Meeticulous issues proper GST tax invoices. Here's what's on them and where to find yours.

## What you get

A GST tax invoice, not just a payment receipt. Each invoice carries:

- The supplier's **GSTIN** and a serial numbered per **Indian financial year** (April–March).
- The correct tax split: **CGST + SGST** when you and the supplier are in the same state, or **IGST** when you're in different states.
- The **place of supply**, resolved from your GSTIN or state.

## What gets an invoice

- Your **Pro subscription** payments.
- Your **bookings** (and digital products) where tax applies.

## Where to find yours

- **Your own invoices:** when you're signed in, your subscription and booking invoices are listed in your account.
- **A specific booking:** open the booking from your dashboard to view its invoice.
- **Emailed link:** GST tax invoices are also available through a secure link we email you, so you can view or download the PDF.

## Adding or fixing your GSTIN

The tax on your invoice (CGST/SGST vs IGST) follows your GSTIN or state, so make sure your details are correct before you need the invoice for filing. If something on an issued invoice looks wrong — the GSTIN, the tax split, or the place of supply — reach us from the [Help](/help) page and we'll sort it.

## Still stuck?

If you can't find an invoice you're expecting, ask here or from the [Help](/help) page and we'll pull it up for you.
