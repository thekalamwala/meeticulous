# Can I use my own domain?

Not yet — a fully custom domain (like `yourbrand.com`) isn't supported today. Here's what you can do instead.

## Your booking link

Your page lives at your Meeticulous link: `meeticulous.com/your-name`. That `your-name` is your username, and it's what you share with clients — on your bio, your site, your socials, or over WhatsApp.

Pick something clean and recognizable: usernames are **3–30 characters**, using lowercase letters, numbers, or hyphens. See [Your username and booking link](/help/docs/your-username-and-booking-link) for how to choose and change it.

## What's not possible yet

- **Pointing your own domain** (`yourbrand.com` or a subdomain) at your Meeticulous page.
- **Hiding the `meeticulous.com` part** of your link.

Custom domains aren't on offer today. If that's important for your brand, tell us from the [Help](/help) page — it helps us prioritise.

## The closest thing today

Make your username your brand name (if it's free), and use that clean `meeticulous.com/your-name` link everywhere. Because a released username is retired and can't be reused, grabbing the one you want early is worth it.
