# What Book A Sloth doesn't do (yet)

We'd rather tell you straight than have you find out later. Here's what Book A Sloth doesn't support today, and what to do instead.

## Is there a mobile app?

Not yet — there's no dedicated iOS or Android app to download. The web app works well in a mobile browser, so you can manage bookings and your page from your phone; it's just not an installable app.

## Do you integrate with Zapier, or have an API?

Not currently. The one integration Book A Sloth supports today is Google Calendar. There's no Zapier, webhook, or public API yet for connecting other tools.

## Can my clients pay from outside India?

Book A Sloth is built India-first: payments run through Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking) and sign-in uses an Indian mobile number for the OTP code. International guest payments aren't a focus today, so it works best when your clients are in India.

## Do you support a team or multiple staff?

Not yet — Book A Sloth is single-host today: one person's services, calendar, and payouts. Multi-staff scheduling (several team members, round-robin assignment) isn't available. If you run a studio or clinic with several practitioners, it's on the roadmap but not shipped yet.

## Do you sync with Outlook or iCloud, or pull in my other calendars?

Google Calendar is fully two-way: confirmed bookings are pushed to your Google Calendar (and updated or removed when they change), and your existing Google busy times are read back in so you're never double-booked. The limitation is other providers — Book A Sloth doesn't yet sync with Outlook, iCloud, or non-Google calendars.

## Something else?

If you're not sure whether something is supported, just ask — or reach us from the [Help](/help) page and we'll give you a straight answer.
