# Reminders, notifications, and badges

## Booking notifications

Confirmations, reschedules, and cancellations go out automatically by **WhatsApp and email** — to you and to your guest — whenever a booking changes. Nothing to set up; it happens on every booking.

For these to land, keep your WhatsApp number verified and your guests' contact details correct.

## In-app notifications (the bell)

Beyond email and WhatsApp, Meeticulous shows notifications in-app on a **bell icon** — in your dashboard if you're a host, and in the top navbar if you're a guest. It updates in real time and collects things like new bookings, reschedules and cancellations, KYC decisions, subscription problems, and referral rewards. Some notices **clear themselves** once you've dealt with them — for example, a KYC-rejected notice disappears once you're approved.

## Reminders before a session

Automated reminders go out before a session to cut down no-shows. **How many reminder points you get depends on your plan:**

- The free tier doesn't include automated reminders.
- Starter includes a 24-hour reminder before the session.
- Pro adds a 1-hour reminder and reschedule alerts.

See the [pricing](/pricing) page for exactly what each plan includes.

## Badges and verification

Badges appear on your public page to build trust. Most are awarded automatically as you hit milestones — this runs on a schedule, so a newly earned badge can take a little while to show up. Some badges are awarded by our team.

If a badge you expected hasn't appeared, give it some time first. For verification questions, reach us from the [Help](/help) page.

## Notifications — common questions

**What notifications does Meeticulous send?** Booking confirmations, reschedules, cancellations, payment confirmations, refund updates, and (on paid plans) session reminders — over **email, WhatsApp, and the in-app bell**. Both guests and hosts get the ones relevant to them. They're triggered automatically when the related action happens.

**Do reminders go out for every booking?** Confirmations do, always. **Reminders are plan-based** — Starter includes the 24-hour reminder, and Pro adds the 1-hour reminder plus reschedule alerts. See [pricing](/pricing).

**Do free bookings still get notifications?** Yes. A ₹0 booking gets the same confirmation as a paid one.

**Will I get a WhatsApp confirmation?** Where WhatsApp is available for the event, yes — some notifications go over both channels, others over one.

**I didn't get my notification.** Check your inbox *and* spam/junk folder, and confirm the email address and phone number on your account are correct. WhatsApp messages need an active WhatsApp account on your registered number. Delivery can occasionally take a few minutes.

**Why did I get a duplicate?** Occasionally a retry attempt can deliver twice. If duplicates keep happening, contact Support.

**A notification failed to send — is my booking affected?** No. Notification delays or failures never change your actual booking or payment status. Failed emails are retried automatically through the send queue.

**How do I change where notifications go?** Update your email address and phone number on your [Profile](/profile). Future notifications use the new details.

**Can I turn off all notifications?** Essential transactional messages (booking, payment, refund) are sent automatically so you stay informed. Marketing/newsletter emails are separate and can be unsubscribed — see [Your profile and email preferences](/help/docs/your-profile-and-email-preferences).

**Are notification templates and providers managed by me?** No — email/WhatsApp templates and providers are configured by Meeticulous administrators, not individual hosts.

**Still missing important notifications?** With correct contact details and spam checked, contact Meeticulous Support and include your booking ID so they can verify whether the notification was generated.
