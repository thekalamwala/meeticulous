# Troubleshooting and error messages

Quick fixes for the errors and hiccups you might run into. Still stuck after trying these? Reach us from the [Help](/help) page.

## OTP and sign-in

**I'm not getting the OTP.** Check WhatsApp and your email inbox (including spam). Confirm the number/email is the one on your account, wait a minute, then request a new code. More on this in [OTP problems](/help/docs/otp-problems).

**"Invalid OTP".** Enter the *latest* code exactly as received — expired or already-used codes won't verify. If unsure, request a fresh one.

**My OTP expired.** Request a new one and use the most recent code within its validity window (codes are short-lived).

**"Too many OTP requests".** You've hit the request limit. Wait for the cooldown to finish before asking for another code.

**I can't log in.** Use the right door for your account: **guests** sign in with their **WhatsApp number + OTP** (no password); **hosts** sign in with **Google**. There's no email/password login, so an "invalid credentials" message usually means the wrong method or the wrong account.

**I can't log in with my phone.** Use the exact number registered to your account — that number *is* your guest identity.

**I can't log in with Google.** Use the same Google account you signed up with. A different Google account creates/looks for a different profile.

**Login keeps failing.** Check your connection and that you're using the correct method above. If it persists, contact Support.

## Account and username

**"Username already exists".** Someone else has that name — pick another. It must be unique, 3–30 characters, lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only.

**I can't save my username.** Make sure it meets those rules and isn't reserved or already taken.

**"Email/phone already registered".** That contact is already linked to an account. Sign in to that account instead of creating a new one — or contact Support if you think it's a mistake.

## Payments, bookings, and slots

**My payment failed.** Nothing was charged for a failed attempt — just try again. See [Refunds and failed payments](/help/docs/refunds-and-failed-payments).

**My payment is still pending.** Verification can take a minute — wait for the status to update rather than paying again.

**Payment succeeded but my booking isn't confirmed.** Give verification a short while to catch up. If the booking still doesn't appear, contact Support with the amount and date — don't rebook.

**My booking isn't in My Bookings.** Refresh, and confirm the booking actually completed. Check the [My Bookings](/my-bookings) list for its status.

**No available slots / all slots booked.** The host has no open times for that date — try another date. Slots reflect the host's working hours minus what's booked and blocked.

## Calendar and services (hosts)

**Google Calendar isn't syncing / busy times aren't blocked.** Reconnect Google Calendar from your dashboard and make sure the calendar permission is granted — authorization can expire. See [Availability and Google Calendar](/help/docs/availability-and-google-calendar).

**My service isn't visible / customers can't find it.** Check the service is active and published, and that your profile is public.

## Uploads and downloads

**I can't upload a file / upload keeps failing.** Check the file meets the format and size limits, and your connection is stable, then retry. Don't refresh mid-upload.

**My download link expired.** Secure product download links expire and have a download limit. For a fresh link, contact the host or Support. See [Buying digital products](/help/docs/buying-digital-products).

## Subscriptions and payouts (hosts)

**My plan/subscription isn't active after paying.** Activation can lag payment slightly — wait for the status to update. If it stays inactive, contact Support.

**My payout is delayed / not received.** Payouts are reviewed and approved before processing, and require approved KYC. Check the request status on your dashboard. See [Getting paid](/help/docs/getting-paid) and [KYC and identity verification](/help/docs/kyc-and-verification).

## Notifications

**I didn't get a notification / confirmation / reminder.** Check spam, confirm your email and phone are correct, and note that reminders depend on your plan. You can also check the **notification bell** in your dashboard (hosts) or navbar (guests) — it shows recent alerts even if an email or WhatsApp message was missed. See [Reminders, notifications, and badges](/help/docs/reminders-notifications-and-badges).

## Site and page errors

**"Internal Server Error" / "Something went wrong" / unexpected error.** A temporary hiccup — refresh and retry after a moment. If it keeps happening, contact Support.

**"Session expired" / logged out suddenly.** Inactive sessions expire for security — just sign in again.

**"Access denied" / "Permission denied".** Your account doesn't have access to that page or action (some features are plan- or role-gated).

**"Page not found (404)".** The URL may be wrong, or the page moved or was removed — check the address or navigate from the menu.

**Blank page after login / slow or not loading.** Check your connection and refresh; if a page is blank, sign out and back in. If a feature won't respond, refresh and retry.

## Still stuck?

Contact Book A Sloth Support with what you were doing, the exact error text, and a screenshot if you have one — it helps us fix it faster.
