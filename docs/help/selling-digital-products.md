# Selling digital products

Alongside bookable services, you can sell digital products — guides, templates, recordings, anything that's a file.

## Add a product

In your dashboard, go to **Products**, upload your file, and set a price (or make it free). It then appears on your page for guests to buy.

When someone buys, they get a secure download link automatically. Each link allows up to **3 downloads** and **expires after 24 hours**, so buyers are encouraged to save the file once they have it.

## How many products can I sell?

The number of digital products depends on your plan — the free tier includes a small number, and paid plans include more, up to unlimited on the top tier. See the [pricing](/pricing) page.

## A buyer says their link expired or hit the limit

Re-issue a fresh download link for them from your Products area. Links are capped at 3 downloads on purpose; a new link resets that.

## Upload won't go through

Files must be under **2 MB**. If an upload is rejected, check the file size and that it's a supported file, then try again. Reach us from the [Help](/help) page if it keeps failing.

## What are Digital Products on Book A Sloth?

Digital Products let hosts sell or share downloadable files — eBooks, templates, guides, PDFs, courses, checklists, or other resources. Customers buy or claim them online and receive a secure download link.

## How do I create a Digital Product?

Go to **Dashboard → Products**, upload your file, add a title and description, set a price (or make it free), and publish. It then appears on your public profile.

## Can I offer a Digital Product for free?

Yes. Free products download without payment; paid products need a completed payment before the download link is issued.

## How do customers download a Digital Product?

After purchasing (or claiming a free product), customers receive a secure, token-protected download link.

## Is there a limit on downloading a Digital Product?

Yes. Each secure link allows up to 3 downloads and **expires after 24 hours**. If it expires or the limit is reached, contact the creator for a new link.

## Can I sell paid Digital Products?

Yes. Set a price before publishing; customers pay before receiving the secure download link.

## What types of Digital Products can I upload?

Downloadable resources such as eBooks, PDFs, templates, guides, worksheets, checklists, and presentations.

## Where can customers find my Digital Products?

Published products appear on your public profile, where visitors can view details and buy or download them.

## Can I edit a Digital Product after publishing it?

Yes. Update the title, description, price, or replace the file any time.

## Can I change the price of my Digital Product?

Yes. New customers see the updated price; existing buyers aren't affected.

## Can I make a paid product free?

Yes. Set the price to ₹0 so customers can download it without paying.

## Can I change a free product into a paid product?

Yes. Update its price before publishing the change; future customers pay before downloading.

## Is my Digital Product secure?

Yes. Secure tokenized download links help protect files from unauthorized access and sharing.

## Can anyone download my Digital Product?

No. Only customers who purchased or claimed it receive a secure download link.

## Can I upload more than one Digital Product?

Yes, depending on your plan's limits. Higher plans allow more.

## How many Digital Products can I create?

It depends on your subscription plan — see the [pricing](/pricing) page. If you hit the limit, upgrade to add more.

## Can I replace the uploaded file after publishing?

Yes. Replace the file so future customers get the latest version.

## Where are my Digital Product files stored?

Files are uploaded and stored securely. Customers receive protected download links instead of direct file access.

## Can I delete a Digital Product?

Yes, from your dashboard. Deleted products no longer appear on your profile.

## What happens if I delete a Digital Product?

It's removed from your profile and new customers can't buy or download it. Consider this carefully if it has active buyers.

## Can customers download a product multiple times?

Yes, within the allowed limit — up to 3 downloads per secure link before it expires.

## Can customers download the product on multiple devices?

Yes, using the secure link, provided it hasn't expired and the download limit isn't reached.

## What happens if the download link expires?

Contact the creator for a new download link.

## What happens if all download attempts are used?

The secure link stops working. Contact the creator if you need access again.

## Can customers share the download link with others?

No. Links are only for the buyer; sharing can use up the available downloads and lock the original buyer out.

## Can I duplicate a Digital Product?

If the Duplicate feature is available, copy an existing product, then edit the title, description, price, or file before publishing.

## Can I upload a thumbnail for my Digital Product?

Yes. A thumbnail makes your product more attractive and shows customers what they're buying.

## Can I update the description of my Digital Product?

Yes, any time. Clear, current descriptions help customers understand what they'll receive.

## Can I change the title of my Digital Product?

Yes. Use a clear, descriptive title so customers can identify it.

## Can I temporarily hide a Digital Product?

If activation/deactivation is available, you can hide a product without deleting it.

## Why isn't my Digital Product visible on my profile?

Make sure it published successfully and you haven't exceeded your plan limits. If it continues, contact Support.

## Can I sell the same Digital Product to multiple customers?

Yes. Each buyer gets their own secure download link.

## How do customers pay for a Digital Product?

Through the secure Razorpay payment gateway. On success, they get access to the download link.

## What happens if a customer's payment fails?

The purchase isn't completed and no link is generated. The customer can retry from the product page.

## What if a customer pays but doesn't receive the download link?

Confirmation can take a few minutes. If it's still missing after payment is verified, contact Support with the payment details.

## Can customers purchase the same Digital Product again?

Yes. Each successful purchase generates a new secure download link.

## Can I see who purchased my Digital Product?

Yes. Your dashboard shows product purchases and related transaction information.

## Can I track Digital Product sales?

Yes. Monitor purchases and earnings from your dashboard.

## Are Digital Product earnings included in my earnings dashboard?

Yes. Payments for Digital Products contribute to your earnings, viewable from your dashboard.

## Is there any platform commission on Digital Product sales?

Platform rules apply to transactions. For questions on commissions or payouts, check your earnings dashboard or contact Support.

## Can I request a payout for my Digital Product earnings?

Yes. Once your eligible balance meets the minimum withdrawal, submit a payout request from your dashboard.

## What happens if I upload the wrong file?

Replace it as soon as possible so future customers get the correct version.

## Can I organize my Digital Products?

Yes. Clear titles, descriptions, and thumbnails make your offerings easy to browse.

## Can customers leave reviews for Digital Products?

If product reviews are supported, customers can leave feedback after purchase. Otherwise, reviews are tied to completed service bookings.

## Can I sell updated versions of the same Digital Product?

Yes. Replace the file or create a new version so customers get your latest content.

## What should I include in my Digital Product description?

What it contains, who it's for, the format, and what customers get after downloading.

## How can I increase Digital Product sales?

Use a professional thumbnail, a clear description, an appropriate price, and keep your profile complete.

## What should I do if customers can't download my product?

Ask them to check whether the link expired or hit the limit. If it continues, contact Support.

## Can I offer Digital Products alongside my services?

Yes. Offer both appointment-based services and downloadable products from the same profile.

## Who should I contact if I have problems managing my Digital Products?

If you can't create, edit, upload, publish, or manage products, contact Book A Sloth Support with your registered email or phone number and a description of the issue.
