# Refunds and failed payments

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, you are **not charged** — no money is kept for a failed attempt. Just try booking again. If your bank shows a pending hold against a failed payment, it reverses on its own.

## Refunds

Refunds are handled case by case, together with the host. There's no one-click refund button, because whether a refund applies depends on the host's terms and the situation.

To request one, have your **booking ID and the reason** ready and reach us from the [Help](/help) page. We'll check eligibility with the host and let you know the status and timeline. Once a refund is processed, the booking shows as refunded.

Our [refund policy](/refund) page has the general terms.

## Money left my account but I have no booking

If you were charged but don't see a confirmed booking, don't book again straight away — contact us from the [Help](/help) page with the amount and the date. We'll trace the payment and either confirm the booking or arrange the refund.

## Cancellations and refunds — common questions

**How do I cancel?** Open the booking on your [My Bookings](/my-bookings) page and choose **Cancel**. Only the person who booked (or the host) can cancel. See [Reschedule or cancel a booking](/help/docs/reschedule-or-cancel).

**Does cancelling refund me automatically?** No. Cancelling changes the booking status but doesn't trigger an automatic refund — there's no one-click refund. Whether a refund applies depends on the host's terms and the timing, and it's handled case by case.

**Will the host be notified if I cancel?** Yes — cancellations, like reschedules and confirmations, notify both sides automatically.

**Can a host cancel my booking?** Yes, if they can't provide the service. You're notified, and any eligible refund is arranged.

**How do I request a refund / check its status?** Reach us from the [Help](/help) page with your **booking ID and reason**. We check eligibility with the host and tell you the status and timeline. Once processed, the booking shows as refunded.

**How is the refund paid and how long does it take?** Back to your original payment method where applicable; timing depends on the payment provider and your bank.

**Can a refund be declined?** Yes. Requests that don't meet the host's terms or the [refund policy](/refund) may not be approved.

**Can I cancel a completed or in-progress booking?** No — only upcoming bookings can be cancelled or rescheduled. Completed bookings can't be cancelled.

**I missed my appointment.** No-shows are handled under the host's cancellation and refund terms — contact us with your booking ID if you need help.

**Can I restore a cancelled booking?** No. Cancelled bookings can't be reopened — book a new time instead. Your booking history still shows the cancelled one.

**Reschedule instead of cancelling?** Often better — your **first reschedule is free** (some hosts charge ₹10 from the second onward). Cancelling ends the booking; rescheduling keeps it and your payment. See [Reschedule or cancel a booking](/help/docs/reschedule-or-cancel).
