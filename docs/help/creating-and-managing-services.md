# Creating and managing services

A service is anything bookable you offer — a session, a class, a consult, a slot.

## Add a service

In your dashboard, go to **Services → Add New** and fill in:

- **Title and description** — what it is.
- **Price** — paid services have a **₹100 minimum**; or set it free (₹0), up to your plan's free-service limit.
- **Duration** — how long it runs, which drives your available slots.
- **Inclusions, exclusions, and FAQs** — so guests know exactly what they're booking.
- **Thumbnail** — an image for the service.

Save it, and it appears on your public page ready to book.

## Edit, duplicate, or pause

From your Services list you can edit a service, duplicate one to reuse its setup, or deactivate one to take it off your page without deleting it. Reactivate any time.

**Editing the price doesn't change existing bookings** — anyone who already booked keeps the price they paid. New bookings use the new price.

## How many services can I have?

The number of services (and how many can be free) depends on your plan. If you hit your plan's limit, you'll be prompted to upgrade — see [Plans and upgrading](/help/docs/plans-and-upgrading).

## A service isn't showing on my page

Check that it's **active**, and that your profile is complete. If a service is deactivated it won't appear to guests.

## What is a service on Book A Sloth?

A service is an appointment or session offered by a host that customers can book online. Each service includes a title, description, duration, price, inclusions, and available booking times.

## How can I view available services?

Visit a host's public profile to browse all the services they currently offer, with details, pricing, and booking options before you reserve.

## Can I book any service I see?

Yes. If the service is active and has available time slots, you can book it directly from the host's profile.

## How do I know what a service includes?

Each service has a detailed description. Many hosts also list inclusions, exclusions, and FAQs so you know exactly what you'll receive.

## Can I compare different services?

Yes. Browse multiple services on a host's profile and compare descriptions, duration, pricing, and features before deciding.

## Are all services paid?

No. Hosts can offer both free and paid services. The price is shown before you book.

## How do I know the price of a service?

The price is displayed on the service page before booking, so you always know the cost before paying.

## Can the service price change?

Yes. Hosts can update prices, but bookings already confirmed keep the original price paid at the time of booking.

## Can I see how long a service lasts?

Yes. Every service shows its duration so you know how much time to set aside.

## Can a service have FAQs?

Yes. Hosts can add FAQs to each service to answer common questions before booking.

## Can I contact the host before booking a service?

Review the service details on the host's profile first. If you still have questions, use the communication options provided by the platform or the host.

## Why can't I book a particular service?

The service may be inactive, unavailable, or out of time slots. Try another service or check back later.

## Can a host temporarily disable a service?

Yes. Hosts can activate or deactivate services any time without deleting them.

## What happens if a service is removed?

New customers can't book a removed or deactivated service. Existing confirmed bookings are handled according to platform policies.

## Can I see customer reviews for a service?

Reviews appear on the host's profile after customers complete bookings, helping you decide.

## Can hosts offer multiple services?

Yes, depending on the limits of their subscription plan.
