# What is Meeticulous?

Meeticulous is a booking-and-payments platform for people who sell their time — coaches, tutors, consultants, therapists, freelancers, and creators.

## What it does

You set up your services once, share one link, and clients pick a time and pay in a single flow. Your calendar fills itself in, your client list builds itself, and confirmations go out automatically. Instead of juggling a WhatsApp thread for scheduling, a calendar invite by hand, and a payment you have to chase, it's one page that handles all three.

## Who it's for

- **Hosts (creators):** independent professionals who take bookings and get paid directly — solo coaches, tutors, consultants, and small studios.
- **Guests (clients):** the people booking those services. Guests need only a phone number to book — no account to create.

## How it's different

- **Get paid at the moment of booking**, not chased for it afterward.
- **Phone-first for guests, Google for hosts** — identity that matches how people actually work, no extra passwords.
- **0% commission on Pro** — on the Pro plan, you keep the full price your client pays.
- **Built for India** — payments through Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking), and confirmations on WhatsApp and email.

## Getting started

Guests: just open a host's link and book. Hosts: start free from the [Become a Host](/become-a-host) page. See [Plans and upgrading](/help/docs/plans-and-upgrading) for what each plan includes.
