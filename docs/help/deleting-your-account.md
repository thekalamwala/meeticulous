# Deleting your account

You can delete your Meeticulous account and your data — there just isn't a self-serve "delete" button yet, so it's handled by our team.

## How to delete your account

Reach us from the [Help](/help) page and ask to delete your account. Tell us the account (your username or the phone number / email you use), and the team will remove it for you.

## What gets removed

Your profile, your services, and your public booking page come down, along with the personal data tied to your account.

A few things we're required to keep: records needed for **tax and legal reasons** — for example, GST invoices already issued and the financial records behind completed payments — are retained as the law requires, even after the account is closed. We'll tell you exactly what's removed and what has to stay.

## Before you delete

- If you have **upcoming bookings**, let your clients know — deleting your account takes your page and services offline.
- If you just want to **stop taking new bookings** for a while rather than delete everything, say so when you contact us and we'll point you at the right option.

## Guests

If you booked as a guest and want your data removed, the same route applies: ask from the [Help](/help) page and we'll take care of it.
