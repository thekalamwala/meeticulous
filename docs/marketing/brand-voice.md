# Brand voice

> **DRAFT** seed for the agent network. Anything written to customers follows this file.
> Visual brand, tokens, components and logo rules live in `design-system/BRAND_RULES.md`
> ("Loop & Slot") — this file is only the **written** voice. Confirm with the founder.

## Who we write for
Indian professionals who take appointments and collect payments: coaches, consultants,
creators, CAs, salons, clinics, studios, freelancers, small businesses. Busy, practical,
price-aware, often on a phone.

## Voice in one line
Plain, warm, and exact — like a competent friend who runs their own business, not a SaaS brochure.

## Do
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Concrete numbers and real benefits, every one from `product-facts.md`.
- Indian English and ₹. "Booking", "host", "guest" — the product's own words.
- Say the honest trade-off (e.g. "staying free keeps you more money below ~₹6,000/month").
- Lead with the customer's problem, then the fix.

## Don't
- Invent proof, testimonials, counts, ratings or results (level N).
- Promise unbuilt features or dates.
- Hype words: "revolutionary", "seamless", "world-class", "game-changer".
- Unnecessary em dashes, keyword stuffing, generic AI filler, repetitive intros.
- Quote a price that isn't in `product-facts.md`.

## Where the founder speaks personally
Social posts and announcements in the founder's name are written for the founder to publish,
in first person, and are level P (founder approves before anything goes public).
