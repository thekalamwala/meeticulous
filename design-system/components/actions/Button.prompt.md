Pill-shaped action button — use for every primary/secondary action; one primary per view.
```jsx
<Button>Get your booking link</Button>
<Button variant="secondary" iconLeft={<Icon name="calendar-plus" />}>Add to calendar</Button>
<Button variant="onBrand">Start free</Button>  {/* on maroon grounds */}
```
Variants: primary · secondary · ghost · onBrand · danger. Sizes s (36) / m (44) / l (52). `block` stretches to full width. Labels are sentence case, verb-first.
